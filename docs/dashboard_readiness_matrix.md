## Relationship and DAX requirements

- Use unique dimension tables with single-direction one-to-many relationships into compatible facts. Do not join any two aggregate facts directly, or infer compatibility because both contain a route or currency label.
- Daily facts have no route/currency/party keys. A route slicer cannot legitimately filter daily totals. Likewise profiles cannot respond to dimensions they do not contain. Disable interactions or explicitly label global/nonresponsive visuals.
- Cube means are `SUM(duration_seconds_sum)/SUM(n)`; label rates are `SUM(labeled_n)/SUM(n)`. Neither the mean of means nor mean of rates is safe without n weights.
- Histogram route denominators repeat across bands. Compute a route denominator with the duration-band filter removed, not SUM(route_denominator_n). Threshold-table TOTAL repeats across thresholds; require one selected threshold.
- Fixed adjusted-effect rows cannot be recomputed by slicers. Treat them as disconnected evidence cards with the original support specification.
- Never compute a filtered median/P95 from cell medians/P95 values. Display exact-grain values or prepare approved distribution summaries/facts for supported filters.
- All facts describe the same source population in alternative views; unioning them double counts. The suppression rollup preserves overall count but not allocation after filters.
- The audited final rule register has the required schema. It is not yet wired into09 and must replace the legacy13-row evidence table after review. Narrative status fields should not become numeric transaction measures.
- Stable CSV filenames inside versioned directories do not by themselves constitute a stable release. Pin run IDs, source/code/artifact hashes and a schema contract before Power BI import.
